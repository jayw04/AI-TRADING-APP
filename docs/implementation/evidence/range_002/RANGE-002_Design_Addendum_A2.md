**A2.3A Three questions kept separate.** (1) RUN TERMINATION: a terminal P5 verdict, or the approved maximum observation period, closes the current run and terminates its observation authority; same-run continuation inside the preregistered schedule consumes no attempt. (2) LINEAGE ELIGIBILITY: whether the research lineage may continue after a terminal P5 verdict; a terminal verdict does not by itself retire the lineage; governing rule UNRESOLVED. (3) NEW-ATTEMPT AUTHORIZATION: whether a new registration or run may start; a terminal verdict does not by itself permit one; it needs separate authorization, an available lineage budget and the applicable recovery rules; governing rule UNRESOLVED. Terminology: **evaluation eligibility** is whether a P5 run qualifies for a sufficiency assessment at a preregistered checkpoint; **lineage eligibility** is whether the research lineage remains eligible for a separately authorized new attempt following a terminal outcome. The two must not be conflated. P6 needs independent, verified human authorization (never implied). **[OT]**; the governing rules for (2) and (3) are pending governance approval and unresolved.
