## E1 — corrected disposition

**`S8.6-EXECUTION-CONTRACT-CUSTODY-001` — SUPERSEDED BY NEWLY LOCATED CUSTODY EVIDENCE.**

The original finding held that the numbered 1–12 S8.6 procedure was **not in Git custody**. That is
**incorrect as stated**. `docs/design/LOW-PIT/…_Execution_Design_Implementation_v0.5.md` (43,450 B) is
present on **`origin/main`**, committed by **#682 (`cd42d489`)** and refined at `8bcb8968`. Its **§7.2
"S8.6 — the twelve checks (v0.4 §11, expanded)"** *is* the numbered procedure. Track-A step **A2 was
executed.**

**Two traps produced the false absence, and both are worth keeping:**

1. **Local `main` was behind `origin/main`.** The document is ABSENT on local `main`, PRESENT on
   `origin/main`. Custody must be measured against the remote.
2. **The document's own custody banner is a fixed point.** v0.5 still carries
   *"⚠ CUSTODY: this document is UNCOMMITTED"* — a pre-commit self-description that A2 never updated.
   **Never read custody from inside the artifact; read it from `git`.**

**What survives, and it is the half that mattered.** The gap is **temporal and operational, not
custodial**: v0.5 was committed **2026-08-25**; the historical S8.6 execution was **2026-08-23**. A
contract committed after an execution cannot retroactively govern it. Therefore **historical check 2
still cannot honestly be adjudicated PASS or FAIL**, and the original ruling's operative conclusion
stands on its own reasoning. ⛔ Do not write `S8.6 CHECK-2-DEPLOYMENT-PIN-001`.

holdings"*. Measured 2026-08-31, the live Account-6 book holds **34** nonzero positions, all 34 of
